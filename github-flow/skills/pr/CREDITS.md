# Credits

Thanks to Dex Horthy for `show-me` and to Matt Pocock for `pr`: they improved how this skill's body shows a structural change. What it takes from them, in their words:

- **[`show-me`](https://github.com/humanlayer/skills/blob/main/plugins/show-me/skills/show-me/SKILL.md), by Dex Horthy (HumanLayer).** "Pick the smallest view that makes the key point clear"; the placement rule, "Place each visual next to the short text it supports. Keep only the calls, files, props, states, and boundaries needed…"; "Use `diff` when the point is what changes and the surrounding shape already exists" and "Match the diff shape to the topic"; the call tree and the component tree, "including state and module boundaries that matter", with their examples and their diffs, in `references/views.md`; the call-tree diff is adapted, its unchanged `navigateToSession` line left unmarked.
- **[`pr`](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/engineering/pr), by Matt Pocock.** It carried `show-me`'s menu of views into a PR body, as its own `CREDITS.md` says. From its `SKILL.md`: "Skip all preambles and keep prose brief." From its `docs/engineering/pr.md`: "'Tests are green' on its own is a claim, not a before and after."
